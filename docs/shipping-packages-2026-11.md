## Session and saved address

Saved address is the newest valid US delivery address from this owner's non-test orders (last 100), including legacy address fields. Pickup orders never replace it. Fetch is authenticated, no-store, and never reserves stock. Checkout will not overwrite address edits or automatically reuse a rate.
